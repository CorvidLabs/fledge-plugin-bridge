# Research

The installed files are configuration and instructions consumed by SpecSync, Augur, Attest, Trust, Claude, Codex, Cursor, and Gemini. They do not participate in Kotlin compilation or runtime dispatch. Treating them as meaningful paths prevents future governance changes from bypassing the verified lifecycle.
